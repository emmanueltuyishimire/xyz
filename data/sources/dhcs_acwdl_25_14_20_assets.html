California Department of Health Care Services: Non-MAGI Asset Limits & Transfer Rules

Non-MAGI Medi-Cal Asset Limits (ACWDL 25-14 and ACWDL 25-20):

Effective January 1, 2026, the Medi-Cal Non-MAGI asset limit is reinstated at $130,000 for one person, plus $65,000 for each additional family member ($195,000 for two persons).

Look-Back Period and Asset Transfer Review (ACWDL 25-18):

Under ACWDL 25-18, the standard 30-month look-back period applies to non-exempt property transfers. Asset transfers occurring between January 1, 2024 and December 31, 2025 are not reviewed or subject to transfer penalties. Only non-exempt transfers occurring on or after January 1, 2026 are reviewed for Medi-Cal long-term care eligibility. Because this review began January 1, 2026, the active look-back window is currently growing toward the full 30 months.

Pending SSI Alignment Proposal:

A proposed administrative alignment to reduce Medi-Cal Non-MAGI asset limits to federal Supplemental Security Income (SSI) levels ($2,000 individual / $3,000 couple) remains pending and would take effect no sooner than January 1, 2027 if enacted.
