2026 SOCIAL SECURITY CHANGES FACT SHEET

Social Security Administration

Tax Wage Base:

The maximum amount of earnings subject to the Social Security tax (taxable wage base) is $184,500 in 2026.

Retirement Earnings Test Exempt Amounts:

Under Full Retirement Age: The earnings-test threshold under FRA for 2026 is $24,480 ($2,040/mo). ($1 in benefits is withheld for every $2 in earnings above the limit.)

In the Year of Reaching Full Retirement Age: The earnings-test limit in the year individuals reach full retirement age is $65,160 ($5,430/mo) for 2026. ($1 in benefits is withheld for every $3 in earnings above the limit.)

Beginning with the month an individual reaches full retirement age, there is no limit on earnings.

Supplemental Security Income (SSI) Federal Payment Standard:

SSI Federal Payment Standard for 2026: Individual $994 per month; Eligible Couple $1,491 per month.

SSI Resource Limit: Individual $2,000; Couple $3,000.
