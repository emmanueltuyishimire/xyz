2027 SOCIAL SECURITY CHANGES FACT SHEET

Social Security Administration

Cost-of-Living Adjustment (COLA):

Based on the increase in the Consumer Price Index (CPI-W) from the third quarter of 2025 through the third quarter of 2026, Social Security and Supplemental Security Income (SSI) beneficiaries will receive a 3.6 percent COLA for 2027.

Tax Wage Base:

The maximum amount of earnings subject to the Social Security tax (taxable wage base) is $192,300 in 2027 (up from $184,500 in 2026).

Retirement Earnings Test Exempt Amounts:

Under Full Retirement Age: The earnings-test threshold under FRA for 2027 is $25,560 ($2,130/mo). (The 2026 limit was $24,480.) ($1 in benefits is withheld for every $2 in earnings above the limit.)

In the Year of Reaching Full Retirement Age: The earnings-test limit in the year individuals reach full retirement age is $67,920 ($5,660/mo) for 2027. (The 2026 limit was $65,160.) ($1 in benefits is withheld for every $3 in earnings above the limit.)

Beginning with the month an individual reaches full retirement age, there is no limit on earnings.

Quarter of Coverage:

In 2027, the earnings required to earn one Social Security quarter of coverage (work credit) is $1,970.

Substantial Gainful Activity (SGA):

Substantial Gainful Activity (SGA) for non-blind disabled individuals is $1,770 per month in 2027.

Substantial Gainful Activity (SGA) for blind disabled individuals is $2,950 per month in 2027.

Trial Work Period (TWP):

The monthly earnings amount to qualify for a trial work period month is $1,270 in 2027.

Maximum Benefit at Full Retirement Age:

The maximum monthly Social Security benefit for a worker retiring at full retirement age is $4,188 in 2027.

Supplemental Security Income (SSI) Federal Payment Standard:

SSI Federal Payment Standard for 2027: Individual $1,029 per month; Eligible Couple $1,544 per month.

SSI student child earned income exclusion: monthly limit $2,500; annual limit $10,080 in 2027.

Average Monthly Social Security Benefits (Before and After 3.6% COLA):

All retired workers: $2,095 to $2,170

Aged couple, both receiving benefits: $3,238 to $3,355

Aged widow(er) alone: $1,936 to $2,005

All disabled workers: $1,642 to $1,701
