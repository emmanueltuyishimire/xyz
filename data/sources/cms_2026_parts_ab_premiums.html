2026 Medicare Parts A & B Premiums and Deductibles

Date: November 8, 2025 / Official Release for Calendar Year 2026

Medicare Part B Premium and Deductible:

The standard monthly premium for Medicare Part B enrollees will be $202.90 for 2026, an increase from 2025.

The annual deductible for all Medicare Part B beneficiaries is $283 in 2026.

Medicare Part A Premium and Deductible:

The Medicare Part A inpatient hospital deductible that beneficiaries pay if admitted to the hospital will be $1,736 in 2026.

The Part A daily coinsurance for days 61-90 of hospitalization in a benefit period will be $434 in 2026.

The daily coinsurance for lifetime reserve days will be $868 in 2026.

For skilled nursing facility (SNF) care, the daily coinsurance for days 21 through 100 of extended care services in a benefit period will be $217.00 in 2026.
