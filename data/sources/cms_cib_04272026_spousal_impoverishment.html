CMS Informational Bulletin: 2026 Spousal Impoverishment Standards

Date: April 27, 2026 / Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services

Community Spouse Resource Allowance (CSRA):

Minimum Community Spouse Resource Allowance: $32,532 (effective January 1, 2026).

Maximum Community Spouse Resource Allowance: $162,660 (effective January 1, 2026).

Home Equity Limits for Medicaid Long-Term Care:

Minimum Home Equity Limit: $752,000 in 2026.

Maximum Home Equity Limit: $1,130,000 in 2026.

Maximum Monthly Maintenance Needs Allowance (MMMNA):

Maximum Monthly Maintenance Needs Allowance: $4,066.50 (effective January 1, 2026 through December 31, 2026).

Minimum Monthly Maintenance Needs Allowance (MMMNA) Floor:

Minimum Monthly Maintenance Needs Allowance: $2,705.00 (effective July 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027) for the 48 contiguous states and the District of Columbia. (Alaska and Hawaii differ).

Excess Shelter Standard (Housing Allowance Standard):

Excess Shelter Standard: $811.50 per month (effective July 1, 2026).
